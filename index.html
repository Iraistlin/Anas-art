About Me

Dear Reader,

A little bit about me: I'm an oil painter, based in Texas, and painting is one of my favorite ways to slow down, and bring the ideas in my head to life. I am drawn to soft colors and creating work that feels dreamy and expressive. Every piece I create is a little part of me. I am so happy to be able to share that part with you. Thank you for being here!

With so much love,
-Liz Miranda
